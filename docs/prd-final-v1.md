# 1. Overview

HRSM is a construction company in Bangladesh that needs a modern, professional website. The site presents the company as experienced and trustworthy, shows its services and completed and ongoing projects, and lets prospective clients and partners send project inquiries.

HRSM staff manage projects, services, blog posts, and incoming inquiries through a secure admin dashboard.

# 2. Objectives

1. Build a strong online brand for HRSM.
2. Present HRSM's construction and engineering services.
3. Showcase completed, ongoing, and upcoming projects.
4. Collect project inquiries from new clients and partners.
5. Show the company's experience, expertise, and achievements.
6. Make it easy for clients to contact HRSM.
7. Let HRSM staff keep key content up to date without a developer.
8. Leave room to add new services, features, and languages later.

# 3. Target audience

- Residential and real estate developers
- Commercial and industrial property owners
- Government and private organizations
- Architects and engineers
- Individual property owners
- Corporate clients

# 5. Website structure

**Home** · **About Us** · **Services** (with a page for each service) · **Projects** (with a page for each project) · **Our Team** · **Clients & Partners** · **News & Blog** (with a page for each post) · **Contact Us** · Privacy Policy · Terms

# 6. Page requirements

## 6.1 Home

1. **Header:** HRSM logo, menu (Home, About Us, Services, Projects, Our Team, Contact Us), and a "Get a Quote" button.
2. **Hero banner:** full-width construction video, headline, short introduction, and two buttons: "Explore Projects" and "Get a Quote".
3. **About HRSM:** short overview of the company and its experience, with a "Learn More" button.
4. **Our Services:** service cards with icon, image, and short description, and a "View All Services" button.
5. **Featured Projects:** selected completed, ongoing, and upcoming projects with images and details.
6. **Why Choose HRSM:** quality construction, experienced team, modern technology, on-time delivery, safety and reliability.
7. **Company Statistics:** years of experience, completed projects, ongoing projects, happy clients.
8. **Client Testimonials.**
9. **Call to action:** "Let's Build Something Great Together" with a Contact Us button.
10. **Footer:** company information, quick links, services, contact details, social media links, and copyright.

## 6.2 About Us

Company introduction, history, mission and vision, core values, leadership, strengths, and certifications and achievements.

## 6.3 Services

Each service has its own detail page.

| Service                 | Description                                |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Building Construction   | Residential and commercial buildings       |
| Civil Engineering       | Civil construction and engineering         |
| Industrial Construction | Factories and industrial buildings         |
| Road Construction       | Roads and infrastructure                   |
| Interior & Exterior     | Interior and exterior solutions            |
| Structural Engineering  | Structural design and engineering          |
| Project Management      | Construction project management            |
| Renovation              | Building renovation and remodeling         |

## 6.4 Projects

HRSM's portfolio.

- **Categories:** All, Completed, Ongoing, Upcoming.
- **Tools:** filter by category, search by project name, image gallery.
- **Each project shows:** name, location, type, description, images, status, start date, completion date, and details.
- Every project has its own detail page.

## 6.5 Our Team

Management (Chairman, Managing Director, Project Director), Chief Engineer, architects, civil engineers, project managers, and the technical team. Each member has a photo, name, designation, and short biography.

## 6.6 Clients & Partners

Client logos, business and corporate partners, and client testimonials.

## 6.7 News & Blog

Company news, construction industry updates, project updates, and articles, each with its own page. HRSM staff publish posts from the admin dashboard.

## 6.8 Contact Us

- **Contact details:** office address, phone, email, Google Maps location, and Facebook page.
- **WhatsApp:** a floating chat button on every page.
- **Inquiry form fields:** full name, company name, email address, phone number, service required, project location, estimated budget, and project description.
- **When a form is submitted:** it is saved in the admin dashboard and an email notification is sent to HRSM.

# 7. Admin dashboard

A secure, login-protected area for HRSM staff.

**Overview cards:** total, completed, and ongoing projects; total and new inquiries; total services; total blog posts.

| Module     | What HRSM can do                                                    |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Projects   | Add, edit, delete, publish; set status and dates; manage image galleries |
| Services   | Add, edit, delete, and reorder services; manage service pages       |
| Inquiries  | View inquiries, change status (new, read, replied, closed), delete  |
| Blog       | Write, edit, publish, and delete posts                              |
| Users      | Manage admin users                                                  |

**Access:** the site launches with one Super Admin account. Additional roles (Editor, Project Manager, Inquiry Manager) can be added later.

**Content updated by the development team on request:** Our Team, Clients & Partners, testimonials, About Us text, company statistics, SEO settings, and general site settings. These can be moved into the dashboard later if HRSM wants to manage them directly.

# 8. Design

- **Style:** premium, modern, clean, and corporate.
- **Colors:** based on the HRSM logo. Until the logo is received, we use a navy blue and construction orange palette with white and gray.
- **Look and feel:** professional typography, high-quality construction photography, smooth scrolling, restrained animation, consistent spacing, premium project cards, and clear call-to-action buttons.
- **Performance:** fast loading on mobile networks, including a lightweight fallback image for the hero video.

# 9. Quality requirements

**Performance and compatibility**

- Fast loading with optimized images and code.
- Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Supports current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
- Accessible, following WCAG guidelines.

**Search engine optimization (SEO)**

- Page titles and descriptions, XML sitemap, robots.txt, structured data, image alt text, and canonical URLs.
- Google Analytics and Google Search Console.
- Starting keywords: HRSM Construction Company, Construction Company in Bangladesh, Building Construction Services, Civil Engineering Company, Industrial Construction Bangladesh, Commercial Building Construction.

**Security**

- HTTPS and secure login with encrypted password storage.
- Role-based access control.
- Form validation and spam protection.
- Protection against common attacks (SQL injection, cross-site scripting).
- Secure file uploads.
- Regular database backups.

**Scalability:** the site is built so new features, pages, and languages can be added later.
