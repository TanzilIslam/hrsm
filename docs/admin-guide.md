# 1. Signing in

1. Open your website address and add `/admin` at the end, for example `https://yourdomain.com/admin`.
2. Enter your email and password, then press Sign in.
3. After signing in you see the Overview page with the left menu.

To sign out, click your round initials at the top right and choose Sign out. Always sign out on a shared computer.

**Change your password:** click your initials, choose Account, then fill in the Change password form. Use at least 10 characters. Changing it signs you out on other devices.

# 2. The Overview page

The Overview shows counts at a glance: total, completed, and ongoing projects, total and new inquiries, services, and blog posts.

The left menu has these sections: **Overview, Projects, Services, Inquiries, Blog, Media.** A small orange number next to Inquiries is how many new messages are waiting. Use the arrow at the top left to collapse the menu to icons.

# 3. Projects

Projects are your portfolio on the website.

**Add a project**

1. Projects, then Add project.
2. Fill in the name, location, type, and status (Completed, Ongoing, or Upcoming).
3. Add the start date, and the completion date for completed work (required for Completed).
4. Write a short description (shown on cards) and the full details (shown on the project page). Leave an empty line between paragraphs.
5. Choose a cover image and add gallery images (see the Media section). The first gallery image is shown first; use the arrows to reorder.
6. Switch on Published to show it on the website. Switch on Feature on the homepage to show it in the Featured projects section.
7. Press Create project.

**Edit or delete:** use the pencil to edit and the trash can to delete. A deleted project disappears from the website at once. The images stay in Media.

**Hide without deleting:** turn off the Published switch in the list. The project stays in the admin but is hidden from visitors.

**Find projects:** use the status tabs and the search box (name, location, or type).

# 4. Services

Services appear on the Services page, the homepage, and the footer.

- Add, edit, or delete services the same way as projects. Each has a title, a short description, full details, an icon, an optional cover image, and a Published switch.
- Use the up and down arrows in the list to change the order. The order you set is the order visitors see.
- The page address (slug) is created from the title. You can change it before saving. Changing it later changes the page's web address.

# 5. Blog

Use the Blog for company news, project updates, and articles.

1. Blog, then New post.
2. Enter the title, a short excerpt (shown on the list and in Google results), and the article.
3. Write the article in the Write tab and check it in the Preview tab (formatting codes are listed below).
4. Add a cover image.
5. Choose when to publish: leave the date empty and switch on Published to publish now, or pick a future date and the post goes live on that date (it shows as Scheduled).
6. Press Create post.

Formatting codes for the article:

- ## Heading for a heading
- **bold** around words for bold text
- A dash and a space at the start of a line for a bullet list
- [link text](https://example.com) for a link

# 6. Inquiries

Every message sent through the Contact form is saved here.

- **New** inquiries are highlighted. Opening one marks it as Read.
- Open an inquiry to see all details. Use **Reply by email** or the phone button to respond.
- Set the status to **Read, Replied, or Closed** to keep track of follow-up.
- Use the status tabs and the search box to find messages (name, email, phone, or message text).
- Delete removes an inquiry permanently.

Email alerts for new inquiries need a one-time setup by your developer. Until then, check this page regularly.

# 7. Media (images)

All images are stored in Media and reused across the site.

- **Upload:** press Upload image, choose a JPEG, PNG, or WebP file (up to 4 MB), and write a short description of the picture (for example "Six storey residential building in Dhaka"). The description helps visitors who cannot see the image and helps Google.
- **View:** the eye icon opens the image in a new tab.
- **Delete:** the trash can removes the image. An image that is still used by a project, service, or post cannot be deleted; replace it there first.

**Image tips:** use clear, bright photos. Landscape photos work best. Keep file sizes small by exporting at about 1600 pixels wide.

# 8. What your developer updates

These parts of the website are updated by your developer on request, because they change rarely:

- About Us text, mission, vision, values, history, and certifications
- Our Team members and photos
- Clients, partners, and testimonials
- Company statistics on the homepage (years of experience, projects, clients)
- The homepage banner video
- Office address, phone, email, WhatsApp number, and social media links
- Privacy Policy and Terms

Send the new text, photos, or logos to your developer to have them updated.

# 9. Common questions

**I changed something but the website still shows the old version.** Wait a few seconds and refresh the page. If it still shows the old version, sign out and in again, or contact your developer.

**I cannot sign in.** Check your email and password. After several wrong attempts you must wait a minute. If you forgot your password, ask your developer to reset it.

**A project or post does not appear on the website.** Check that its Published switch is on. For blog posts, also check the publish date is not in the future.

**An image will not upload.** Use a JPEG, PNG, or WebP file under 4 MB and add a description.

**I need another person to manage the website.** Ask your developer to add a new admin account.

# 10. Good habits

- Replace placeholder text and images with your own before sharing the website.
- Check each page on your phone after publishing something new.
- Reply to new inquiries quickly and set their status afterwards.
- Never share your password. Change it if you think someone else knows it.
